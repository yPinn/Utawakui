# Utawakui v0.5.1 公開測試版

本次更新補上專輯批次下載與作業完成通知，讓新曲目的歌手資訊立即可用，並改善場次換曲、背景歌詞查詢與 BGM 的直播擷取。

## 新功能

- **專輯批次下載：** 可一次下載專輯中尚未持有的曲目，並停止排隊或重試失敗項目。
- **完成通知：** 一般下載與伴奏處理完成後會顯示通知；批次作業合併成一則摘要，避免逐首重複提醒。
- **BGM 直播擷取：** BGM 沿用演唱選定的擷取輸出裝置，並新增獨立的「直播擷取總音量」，同時調整演唱與 BGM 的擷取音量。

## 改善

- **歌詞查詢：** 減少重複背景查詢，調整重試等待並優先處理手動查詢，降低過於頻繁觸發服務限流的機會。
- **本機檔案檢查：** 加強素材解壓縮與 Windows 檔名的檢查。
- **設定頁整理：** 精簡說明與重複資訊，將低頻管理操作收進選單；實驗歌詞設定聚焦環境與偏好，逐曲操作回到歌曲情境。

## 問題修復

- 修正新下載曲目的歌手資訊可能無法立即點選，必須重啟才更新的問題。
- 修正場次歌曲播完、自動換曲時可能意外回到一般播放佇列的問題。
- 修正按下暫停後，先前仍在準備的播放動作可能又恢復播放的問題。
- 修正切換曲庫、取消或重試作業時，舊進度、完成通知與資料刷新可能影響新作業的問題。
- 修正擷取輸出仍在初始化時，開啟變調後再還原可能保留錯誤音訊路由的問題。

## 升級注意事項

- 新增的「直播擷取總音量」預設為 **50%**，與本機監聽及導唱比例分開。升級後請到音訊設定確認擷取音量，並檢查 OBS 的收音大小。
- 背景歌詞查詢仍受來源服務的可用性與限流影響；本版調整查詢排程，不保證每次查詢都能取得結果。

## 已知限制

- 此公開測試版尚未加入受信任的 Windows 數位簽章，安裝時可能顯示「未知的發行者」。
- 播放時偶發電子音／雜訊仍在調查；本版的變調路由修正不代表此問題已排除。

## 安裝前須知

- 請 **只從** [Utawakui 官方 Releases](https://github.com/yPinn/Utawakui-Releases/releases) 下載。可使用下載頁提供的 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 確認安裝檔是否完整。
- 應用程式會在啟動後及之後每隔數小時 **檢查** 新版本，但 **不會自行下載或安裝**。你可以在設定中關閉自動檢查。
- 覆蓋安裝通常會 **保留** 應用程式設定與自選曲庫；重要的自備素材仍建議先備份。
- 使用者 **須自行確認** 歌曲、歌詞、封面、錄音與其他素材的使用權利。
- 加入 [Discord](https://discord.gg/yJKddEtpNt) 社群，查看公告或回饋想法。

## Utawakui v0.5.1 Public Test

This update adds album batch downloads and completion notices, makes artist information available immediately for newly downloaded tracks, and improves session transitions, background lyrics lookup, and BGM capture for streaming.

## New Features

- **Album batch downloads:** Download tracks you do not yet have from an album, stop queued items, or retry failed items.
- **Completion notices:** Regular downloads and accompaniment processing now report their results. Batch jobs produce one summary instead of repeated notices for every track.
- **BGM stream capture:** BGM uses the capture output device selected for singing. A separate capture master volume now adjusts the captured level of both singing playback and BGM.

## Improvements

- **Lyrics lookup:** Fewer duplicate background requests, adjusted retry waits, and priority for manual searches reduce unnecessary rate-limit triggers.
- **Local file checks:** Stronger validation of extracted assets and Windows filenames.
- **Settings cleanup:** Shorter explanations and fewer repeated details, with infrequent management actions grouped in menus. Experimental lyrics settings focus on environment and preferences; per-track actions stay with the song.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed artist information for newly downloaded tracks sometimes remaining unclickable until the app restarted.
- Fixed session playback sometimes returning unexpectedly to the regular queue when advancing after a track ended.
- Fixed a pending playback action sometimes resuming playback after you pressed pause.
- Fixed stale progress, completion notices, and refreshes affecting newer jobs after a library change, cancellation, or retry.
- Fixed an incorrect audio route that could remain when pitch was enabled and then reset while capture output was still initializing.

## Upgrade Notes

- The new capture master volume defaults to **50%** and is separate from local monitoring and the guide-vocal balance. After upgrading, check the capture volume in audio settings and confirm the incoming level in OBS.
- Background lyrics lookup still depends on provider availability and rate limits. This update improves scheduling but cannot guarantee a result for every request.

## Known Limitations

- This public test build does not yet have a trusted Windows digital signature, so Windows may show “Unknown publisher.”
- Intermittent electronic noise during playback remains under investigation. The pitch-routing fix does not establish that this issue is resolved.

## Before Installing

- Download **only from** the [official Utawakui Releases](https://github.com/yPinn/Utawakui-Releases/releases). Use the supplied `SHA256SUMS.txt` to check the installer.
- The app **checks** after startup and every few hours, but **does not download or install automatically**. Automatic checks can be disabled in settings.
- Installing over an existing copy normally **preserves** app settings and your selected library. Back up important user-supplied media first.
- Users **must confirm** their rights to songs, lyrics, artwork, recordings, and other materials they use.
- Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/yJKddEtpNt) community for announcements and feedback.
