# Utawakui

> 為歌回與翻唱設計的 Windows 桌面工具。

[繁體中文](#繁體中文) | [English](#english)

## 繁體中文

### 可以做什麼

- **曲庫**：整理電腦裡的歌曲、歌詞與封面。
- **演唱工具**：安排歌單、練唱，演出時切歌、看歌詞。
- **畫面輸出**：透過直播軟體的瀏覽器來源，顯示目前曲目、歌詞與已唱曲目。

### 下載

- 系統需求：Windows 10 以上（64 位元）。
- [下載最新版](https://github.com/yPinn/Utawakui-Releases/releases/latest)。請只從官方下載頁取得；安裝時可能顯示「未知的發行者」，可用版本頁的 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核對檔案。
- 有新版時 App 會提示，是否下載與安裝由你決定。

### 資料與素材

歌曲、歌詞與設定主要保存在你的電腦。需要連網的功能只會連到對應的服務，更新檢查可在設定中關閉。

請使用你有權使用的歌曲、歌詞與封面；Utawakui 不附帶歌曲或相關授權。

## English

### What It Does

- **Library**: Organize the songs, lyrics, and artwork on your computer.
- **Performance**: Plan setlists, practice, and switch songs and follow lyrics while you perform.
- **Output**: Show the current song, lyrics, and a list of songs you’ve already performed through a browser source in your streaming software.

### Download

- Requirements: Windows 10 or later (64-bit).
- [Download the latest version](https://github.com/yPinn/Utawakui-Releases/releases/latest). Download only from the official page. Windows may show "Unknown publisher"; check the file with `SHA256SUMS.txt` on the release page.
- The app tells you when a new version is available; you decide whether to download and install it.

### Your Data and Media

Songs, lyrics, and settings are primarily stored on your computer. Features that need the internet connect only to their own services, and update checks can be turned off in Settings.

Use songs, lyrics, and artwork you are authorized to use. Utawakui does not include songs or licenses for them.
